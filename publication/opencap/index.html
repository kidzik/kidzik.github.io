Publication / 2023

OpenCap: Human movement dynamics from smartphone videos

Scott D. Uhlrich, Antoine Falisse, Łukasz Kidziński, Julie Muccini, Michael Ko, Akshay S. Chaudhari, Jennifer L. Hicks, Scott L. Delp

PLOS Computational Biology

Paper Code Project

OpenCap combines video-based pose estimation, machine learning, and biomechanical simulation to estimate human movement and musculoskeletal forces from two or more smartphone cameras.

Scott D. Uhlrich, Antoine Falisse, and Łukasz Kidziński contributed equally to this work.

Read the published paper for the methods, validation, and study limitations.
