Source: BodyParts3D4.0 IS-A polygon meshes,99% polygon reduction, with official structure labels.
Modifications: unit conversion from millimetres, a shared neutral-pose registration field fitted to MS-Human-700 bone centers and longitudinal axes, bounded positive muscle-surface fitting, curvature-limited centerline cages, distal tendon landmark fitting, and continuous kinematic-frame skinning weights. Represented bones use the original simulator geometry with rigid body transforms; BodyParts3D supplies muscles and additional bone detail. The BodyParts3D license applies to the atlas assets, not as a blanket license for original simulator geometry. Anatomical source geometry is separate from simulated muscle paths. Display deformation is approximate and not validated subject-specific anatomy, actuator correspondence, or muscle-volume conservation. Meshes never participate in training physics.
