Łukasz Kidziński

I’m a co-founder of Gosset, a pharmaceutical intelligence platform. My work there draws on a background in statistics, machine learning, and developing software for healthcare.

My academic work is focused on machine learning and statistics, particularly their applications to biomechanics and health. At EPFL and Stanford, I worked on functional time series, learning analytics, and movement analysis from video. I’ve also brought that research background into healthcare software as a co-founder of Saliency.ai, a medical imaging platform, and a Director of AI at Clario.
