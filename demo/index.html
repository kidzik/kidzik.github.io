The question behind the demo

Can a model adapt the way we do?

In our 2021 review, we asked how reinforcement learning could help us study movement in new conditions. The Learn to Move challenge tested how controllers changed speed, turned and came to a stop.

Here, a controller trained on a human walking reference gets a new test. What happens when a push disturbs its balance?

Read Song et al. (2021) ↗

What to look for

  1. Movement Does the body return toward its original path?
  2. Muscles Where does activation change after the push?
  3. Recovery Does forward motion settle after the disturbance?
